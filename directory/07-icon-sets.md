pages, checklists, and covers: clean strokes, no photographs, no licensing surprises.

## Practical tips

- For printables, prefer stroke icons at consistent 24px or 48px: they scale crisply to
  print at 300 dpi and tint to your palette with any editor.
- For KDP covers, icons pair beautifully with vintage plates (anatomy + medical cross,
  NASA imagery + orbital ring) - keep the strokes thick so they hold at thumbnail size.
- Attribution for CC BY sets (Font Awesome, Game-icons) belongs in your copyright page;
  a single line covers unlimited icons from that set.
