# Search hubs and photo libraries

Discovery tools and open-license photo sites. These are where "CHECK" discipline
matters most: they aggregate many licenses, so the eleven-second habit of reading the
license tag on each image is what keeps your products safe.

| Source | What's inside | License notes | Safe to sell | Link |
|--------|---------------|---------------|--------------|------|
| Openverse | One search box across a billion CC and PD works from museums, Flickr, and Commons; filter by license. Shows attribution strings automatically. | Aggregates open licenses; filter to CC0/PD or read each result's license. | CHECK | https://openverse.org/ |
| Pixabay | Millions of free stock photos, vectors, and videos. Contributor-submitted, so license terms are site-level, not item-level. | Pixabay Content License: commercial use in products allowed; no redistribution "as is" (standalone stock-like use); no model releases for people. | SAFE-COND | https://pixabay.com/ |
| Pexels | High-quality modern stock photos and video, very "cover-friendly". | Pexels License: free for commercial use in products; no redistribution as stock. | SAFE-COND | https://www.pexels.com/license/ |
| Unsplash | Beautiful modern photography with generous terms. | Unsplash License: free commercial use in products; primarily forbids re-selling as competing stock and some logos/trademarks. | SAFE-COND | https://unsplash.com/ |
| PublicDomainPictures.net | Contributor-uploaded images verified as public domain (or CC0). | Site states images are free to use (PD/CC0); check the per-image tag. | CHECK | https://www.publicdomainpictures.net/en/ |
| Openclipart | Classic clipart community; every upload is CC0 by site policy. Not period art, but friendly cartoon style. | CC0 across the whole site. | SAFE | https://openclipart.org/ |
| PICRYL | Public-domain media search engine across many institutions; made for browsing by era and topic. | Site states permission for reuse is not required; verify tags on the item page. | CHECK | https://picryl.com/ |

## Practical tips

- Openverse is the fastest way to find CC0-only work across sources; it also generates
  ready-to-paste attribution lines, which is handy for CC BY finds.
- The "free stock" sites (Pixabay, Pexels, Unsplash) are safe for products like covers
  and printables. What they prohibit is competing with them - don't package their files
  up as your own stock library.
- People in stock photos: KDP distribution is fine, but if your product implies a
  person endorses something, that's a separate (non-license) concern - keep it honest.
