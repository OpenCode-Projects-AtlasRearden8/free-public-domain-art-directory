# Vintage anatomy prints

Anatomy engravings and plates that read as "classic medical illustration" - perfect for
healthcare-adjacent titles (nursing, wellness, medical history), coloring books, and
classroom-style printables. These are education/inspiration only; never use imagery to
imply medical advice.

## Content caveats worth knowing

- Period anatomy art sometimes includes detailed nudity and fetal models. If your book
  is for a homeschool or classroom market, use the skeletal and anatomical-diagram style
  plates rather than the full-figure engravings.
- Never use anatomy art to imply diagnosis, dosage, or medical instruction. Pair it with
  a clear "educational reference" framing on your cover or product page.
