# Library of Congress

The Library of Congress digitizes at museum quality and explicitly curates "Free to Use
& Reuse" sets so you never need to guess. For KDP covers and printables, LOC is one of
the few sources where the licensing question is usually answered before you ask it.

| Source | What's inside | License notes | Safe to sell | Link |

## Practical tips

- LOC serves high-res TIFFs via the item page's "Download" menu. Covers need ~2550 px
  wide minimum; most F2U items clear that easily.
- When in doubt, check the item rights advisory (e.g., "No known restrictions on
  publication"). That phrase on an LOC item page is your green light.
