# NASA imagery

NASA imagery is world-class and effectively copyright-free: as a US government agency,
most NASA-created material is not copyrighted in the US. Covers, interiors, and
printables especially benefit from the high-res originals. Attribution is customary
(and NASA requests its logo treatment be respected), not legally required.

## Practical tips

- Filter images.nasa.gov to "National Aeronautics and Space Administration" as creator
  when you need maximum confidence; third-party contributors' material keeps its own
  tags.
- High-res downloads are normal (original TIFF/PNG on SVS and Photojournal). Covers
  (1590 x 2560 at 300 dpi) need real resolution - NASA delivers.
- Keep the NASA logo out of your design in a way that suggests endorsement. Using the
  imagery is fine; implying NASA approved your book is not.
