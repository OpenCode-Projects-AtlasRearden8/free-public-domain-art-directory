How to read the flags

  • SAFE — Public domain or CC0. Use freely in KDP covers and printables.
  • SAFE-ATTR — Fine for products; include the requested credit line.
  • SAFE-COND — Commercial use allowed under the site's own license conditions.
  • CHECK — Mixed licenses - verify the tag on each image before use.
  • SAFE — Reference page (policy or guidance), not an art source.
  • NO — Not safe to sell with.

NASA imagery

NASA imagery is world-class and effectively copyright-free: as a US government agency, most NASA-created material is not copyrighted in the US. Covers, interiors, and printables especially benefit from the high-res originals. Attribution is customary (and NASA requests its logo treatment be respected), not legally required.
